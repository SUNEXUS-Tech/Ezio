PHOTO · THE MAN
Beyond the title

A man of his word. A heart that gives.

Jude Ifeanyi Anwukah is fondly called Eziokwubundu by friends and family, a name that reflects the honesty, integrity and conviction that define his character.

A man who stands by the truth, he is deeply loved and respected by many for his generosity, kindness and willingness to make a difference in the lives of others.

His impact extends beyond his immediate family. Through his generosity and commitment to investing in young people, particularly within his extended family and community, he has helped nurture hope, opportunity and a sense of belonging.

To those who know him, he is more than a name or a title. He is family, a source of encouragement and a man whose life has touched many.

A good name is a legacy. A generous heart is a blessing.
